## Phase question

- EVT: Can the architecture meet the requirements and retire its largest technical risks?
- DVT: Does the production-intent design meet the specification, user needs, reliability, security, and applicable compliance requirements?
- PVT: Can the intended suppliers, tooling, instructions, programming, calibration, and tests produce conforming units repeatedly?

## Phase-specific review

### EVT

- [ ] Critical interfaces, power, timing, thermal, mechanical, RF, sensor, and control assumptions were measured.
- [ ] Major unknowns have evidence, a bounded experiment, or an explicit owner decision.
- [ ] Architecture, observability, update/recovery, and test access are viable.
- [ ] Failures and rework are traced into the next design revision.

### DVT

- [ ] Production-intent electronics, mechanics, firmware, software, cables, power, antenna, and configuration were tested together.
- [ ] Functional, environmental, reliability, abuse, security, privacy, usability, accessibility, interoperability, and recovery coverage is proportionate to use.
- [ ] Pre-compliance/compliance evidence and technical documentation are on plan.
- [ ] Manufacturing test coverage and service/repair behavior are validated.

### PVT

- [ ] Production suppliers, materials, tooling, fixtures, work instructions, programming, calibration, packaging, and inspection were used.
- [ ] Yield, cycle time, rework, process capability, traceability, and escape/containment handling meet targets.
- [ ] Every unit/lot can be tied to hardware, firmware, configuration, calibration, test results, and approved deviations.
- [ ] Release, rollback/recovery, spares, field diagnostics, support and end-of-life responsibilities are ready.
